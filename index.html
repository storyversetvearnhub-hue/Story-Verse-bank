📚 How To Use StoryVerse Bank

1️⃣ Create Your Account

  1. Enter your email address.
  2. Create a password of at least 6 characters.
  3. Tap Create Account.
  4. Your StoryVerse Bank account number will be generated automatically.

2️⃣ Login

Enter your registered email and password, then tap Login. You can also use Google Login if Google authentication is enabled correctly in Firebase.

3️⃣ Check Your Account Number

After login, open My Account. Your automatically generated StoryVerse Bank account number is your customer reference.

4️⃣ Make a Deposit

  1. Tap Deposit.
  2. The deposit amount is ₦2,000.
  3. Tap Pay ₦2,000 with OPay.
  4. Complete the payment.
  5. Submit your deposit request.
  6. Your request will initially show Pending.
  7. After verification and approval, it can show Approved.

5️⃣ Check Deposit Status

Go to Request Status. You may see:

  • 🟡 Pending — waiting for verification.
  • 🟢 Approved — request has been approved.
  • 🔴 Rejected — request was rejected.
  • 🔵 Processing — request is being processed.

6️⃣ Make a Withdrawal

  1. Tap Withdraw.
  2. Enter the amount.
  3. Enter your bank name.
  4. Enter your 10-digit account number.
  5. Enter the account name.
  6. Submit the request.

The withdrawal will show as Pending until it is reviewed.

7️⃣ Make a Transfer

  1. Tap Transfer.
  2. Enter the recipient's StoryVerse Bank account number.
  3. Enter the transfer amount.
  4. Add a transfer note if necessary.
  5. Submit the transfer request.

8️⃣ Check Transaction History

Tap Transactions to see transactions that have been recorded for your account.

9️⃣ Contact Customer Service
